Delete your Arrived account

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Arrived and tap your picture to open Settings.
  2. Scroll to Privacy & account and tap Delete my data.
  3. Read the list, then tap Delete my data to confirm.

The app deletes your Arrived account on our servers and signs this phone out.

Option 2: by email (if you cannot open the app)

Send an email to bala.code01@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Google account, with the subject Delete my Arrived account.

We will check that the request comes from that address, then delete your account. We aim to finish within 30 days, and will reply to confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Arrived sign-in record, and everything linked to it
  • Your display name and invite code
  • Your places, your private notes, and your nicknames for others
  • The people you added to your places, and the places others added you to
  • Your arrival history and your push notification token
  • Your alert and quiet-hours settings

What is not deleted

  • Your Google account. It stays as it is, and you can still use it for other apps.
  • Copies that may remain in our provider's backups and logs for a limited period, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Please note: deletion is permanent. You cannot undo it. If you sign in to Arrived again later, you start from the beginning, with a new invite code. Other people's own places are not affected.
